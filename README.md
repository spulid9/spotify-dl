# Spotify Downloader

Download your top 200 liked Spotify songs as MP3s with album art and metadata.

## What it does

1. Log into Spotify via your browser (no token saved)
2. Fetches your 200 most recently liked songs
3. Searches YouTube for each song and downloads the audio
4. Tags the file with proper artist, title, album, track number, and album art
5. Caches progress — only downloads new songs on subsequent runs
6. Cleans up orphaned files when you unlike songs

## How to use

1. Download the latest `SpotifyDownloader.exe` from [Releases](https://github.com/spulid9/spotify-dl/releases)
2. Double-click it
3. Your browser opens — click "Login with Spotify" and authorize
4. Click "Scan Library" to see what's new
5. Click "Download New Songs"
6. Find your music in `Downloads/spotify-music/`

No installation, no accounts to create, no terminal.
